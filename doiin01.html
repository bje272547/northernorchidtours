ITINERARY

07:30

Hotel Pickup

Your guide meets you at your hotel lobby in an air-conditioned vehicle.

09:30

Arrive at Summit of Doi Inthanon & Ang Ka trail (45 mins)

Stand at the highest peak in Thailand, 2,565 meters above sea level. Experience the crisp mountain air, stroll along the lush Ang Ka Nature Trail.

10:30

Arrive at Twin pagodas (1 hr)

Marvel at the majestic Twin Royal Pagodas surrounded by vibrant flower gardens and stunning mountain vistas.

11:30

Lunch break (45 mins)

Recharge with a delicious meal and relax before gearing up for your afternoon eco experience.

12:30

Start the Pha Dok Siew Trail and visit Mae Klang Luang Village. (2.5 hrs)

Trek through rich forest trails, admire hidden waterfalls and rice terraces, and visit an authentic Karen hill tribe village.

03:15

Arrive at Wachirathan Waterfall (45 mins)

Witness the thunderous beauty of Wachirathan Waterfall and refresh yourself in its invigorating mountain mist.

04:00

Return back to Chiang Mai city (1.5 hrs)

Sit back and relax on the comfortable drive back to Chiang Mai city, wrapping up a memorable day of nature.

05:30

Drop off at your hotel

Arrive at your hotel safely.
