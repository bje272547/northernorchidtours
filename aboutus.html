About Northern Orchid Tours

The people behind
every journey we craft

A small team of Chiang Mai locals, guides and storytellers, dedicated to showing Northern Thailand at its own unhurried pace.

Our Story

Born in Chiang Mai, shaped by the North

Northern Orchid Tours began with a simple idea: that the North of Thailand is best discovered slowly, in the company of people who actually live here. We are guides, drivers, cooks and hosts who grew up among these mountains, temples and rivers — and who still find something new in them every season.

Rather than moving guests through a checklist of sights, we design each day around quiet moments: a sunrise offering at a hillside temple, a cup of tea with a hill-tribe family, the particular light on Doi Inthanon before the crowds arrive. Every itinerary is built by hand, for a small group or a single family, never mass-produced.

Today, our team looks after journeys across Chiang Mai, Chiang Rai and Mae Hong Son — but the intention has never changed: genuine hospitality, local knowledge, and a slower, more considered way of travelling.

What We Believe

The principles behind every itinerary

Local, Not Outsourced

Every guide and driver is based in the North. What you get is lived-in knowledge, not a script.

Slow by Design

Fewer stops, more time at each one. We would rather you remember three places well than rush through ten.

Respect for Place

From hill-tribe villages to sacred temples, we travel as guests — with consent, care and fair pay to the communities we visit.
